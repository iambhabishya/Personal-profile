A LITTLE ABOUT ME

HELLO I'M
BHABHISHYA.

I'm a frontend developer and UI/UX designer interested in clean interfaces, strong typography and thoughtful interactions. I care about the way a product looks, but I care just as much about the way it feels to use. I enjoy taking a project from a rough idea and turning it into a polished experience. That process usually moves through structure, visual direction, interface design, frontend development, responsiveness and the small details in between. Every stage is a chance to make the final product clearer and more useful.

My approach is practical and curious. I like understanding the problem before choosing a solution, making space for ideas to develop, and then reducing the unnecessary until the important parts feel obvious. Good design should have personality, but it should never make people work harder than they need to. On the development side, I build responsive interfaces with semantic structure, reusable components and attention to performance. On the design side, I think about hierarchy, spacing, typography, user flows and the quiet moments that make an interaction feel considered.

When I'm not building websites, I'm usually experimenting with new interfaces, learning something technical or collecting references for the next idea. I find inspiration in editorial layouts, useful tools, music, architecture and the small design decisions that often go unnoticed when they are done well. Today, I work across design and development to create digital experiences that are expressive without being complicated, polished without losing warmth, and solid underneath the surface.
